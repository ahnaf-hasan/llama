### 🦙 Llama Models

Llama is a family of large language models (LLMs) developed by Meta, empowering developers, researchers, and businesses to build innovative AI applications. It provides a foundation for experimentation, customization, and responsible AI development.

| Llama 3 | 4/18/2024 | 8B, 70B | 8K | TikToken-based |
